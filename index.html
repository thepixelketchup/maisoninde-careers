About Maison Indē

Maison Indē means House of India: a home for colour, craft, stories and the people who make them. We are an artisanal fashion brand working with craft clusters across India to create handcrafted collections using 100% natural fibers. Our pieces are designed in Amsterdam and made in small batches, so every role here is close to the craft and close to the decisions.
